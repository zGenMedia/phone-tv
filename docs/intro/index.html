Let's be straight

Phone TV doesn't give you movies. It gives you one place for them.

It isn't another streaming service, and nothing to watch comes with it. Think of it as the front door: the apps and libraries you already have, behind one screen that's built for leaning back.

What it is

  • A home screen, like the one on a Google TV or Fire TV
  • A launcher for the streaming apps you already use
  • A guide to movies and shows: what's popular, who's in it, what to watch next
  • A place that remembers what you saved and where you left off

What it isn't

  • A streaming service or a subscription
  • A source of free movies or shows
  • A replacement for Netflix, Disney+, Plex or anything else you have
  • Something that needs your email, a card or an account

Fair questions

Before you try it

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account, and no paid tier. It's open source, so anyone can read the code.

So where do the movies come from?

From what you already have: your streaming apps, and your own libraries like Plex. Phone TV shows you what's out there and keeps track of what you're watching. It doesn't host, sell or supply anything to watch.

Do I still need my Netflix or other subscriptions?

Yes. Phone TV opens those apps for you. It doesn't replace them or get around them.

Why isn't it in the app store?

Phone TV is an independent project, downloaded straight from its own site. The step-by-step guide there walks you through installing it.

Does it change my phone?

No. It's an app. Open it when you want the TV screen, close it when you don't, and remove it like any other app.

Do I need a remote?

No. It works by touch. It also works with a TV remote, a game controller or a keyboard if you have one.

What about iPhone?

Not yet. The iPhone version gets built when enough people ask for it. Add your name to the list.
