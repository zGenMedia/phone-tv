Privacy

Your TV, your business.

Phone TV has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. This page lists everything it keeps and every place it talks to. Last updated 17 November 2026.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, your Library (saved titles, saved actors, watch history) and cached artwork and catalog pages.
  • Sign-in tokens for Plex and Trakt, if you connect them. They are kept out of Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Nothing is collected, sold or shared by zGenMedia. We never see any of it.

Where Phone TV connects

  • Cinemeta and Metahub (Stremio's public catalog and artwork) for the movies and shows on the home screen.
  • TVmaze for the cast of TV shows. These requests contain the show's IMDb id.
  • Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia for banner suggestions, cast lists, actor photos and short biographies. These requests contain the titles and names you look at.
  • GitHub Pages (this site) to check for updates and download them.
  • Plex, only if you link it: plex.tv to sign in, then your own Plex server for your libraries, Continue watching and watch history.
  • Trakt, only if you sign in or add a username or lists: to read your watch history, watchlist and public lists. Phone TV only reads from Trakt; it never changes your Trakt data. Signing out removes the token from the device and asks Trakt to cancel it.
  • When you choose to watch something, the app you pick (Stremio, Nuvio, Plex, YouTube) opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Moving to another device

Send my setup passes your Library and settings straight to your other device on the same Wi-Fi, only after its 6-digit code is entered. It never leaves your network. Save setup to a file writes the same to a file you choose. Neither includes sign-ins or tokens.

Trakt

Phone TV uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

Questions

Phone TV is open source, so everything above can be checked in the source code. Ask anything on GitHub issues.
