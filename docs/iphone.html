What Phone TV does

  • One home screen for everything you watchMovies, shows and your apps on one TV-style screen, with a featured banner up top and rows you can arrange.
  • Find something fastBrowse by genre, network or studio, follow an actor through everything they've been in, or let Pick for me choose from what you've liked before.
  • Channels you can tune in toA guide of always-on movie and show channels, for when you'd rather drop in than choose.
  • Your library, your historySave titles, actors and studios, keep your place in what you're watching, and bring your Plex and Trakt along.
  • Share anythingSend a movie, show or actor to a friend. That's probably how you got here.
  • Free, with no ads and no accountOpen source, with no tracking of what you watch.
